## 1. Executive Summary

The SIP website is a developer acquisition and category-building surface. It should launch early — before the platform is fully mature — to establish the SIP brand, attract open-source developers, plugin builders, contributors, and early design partners, and start building search engine and AI search authority.

The website targets competitive software categories (CMMS, CRM, EAM, FSM, work order software, asset management software) while also capturing long-tail searches from industries, careers, businesses, and physical assets that need service or repair.

## 8. Competitive Keyword Strategy

### 8.1 CRM Keywords

Target carefully — SIP is not a sales CRM. Keywords: service CRM, field service CRM, CRM for service companies, CRM for repair companies, CRM for equipment service, open source service CRM, AI service CRM.

Angle: SIP is a service intelligence platform for organizations that need to track physical assets, work orders, service history, documents, and repair decisions. For service businesses, SIP acts as the asset and work-order intelligence layer traditional CRMs don't provide.

### 8.2 CMMS Keywords

Keywords: CMMS, CMMS software, open source CMMS, best open source CMMS, AI CMMS, CMMS API, CMMS with AI, CMMS for developers, CMMS alternative, modern CMMS, plugin-based CMMS.

Angle: SIP overlaps with CMMS but is designed as an AI-first service intelligence platform with a service-domain canonical model, public APIs, plugin extension points, and AI-readable maintenance history.

### 8.3 EAM Keywords

Keywords: enterprise asset management, EAM software, open source EAM, AI enterprise asset management, EAM vs CMMS, asset lifecycle management software, physical asset management software.

Angle: SIP starts with maintenance and service intelligence but its canonical model supports asset lifecycle, work order history, documents, service contracts, and future enterprise workflows.

### 8.4 FSM Keywords

Keywords: field service management software, FSM software, open source field service software, field service work order software, field technician software, AI field service management.

Angle: SIP supports field service through work orders, assignments, technicians, documents, activity logs, and future dispatch workflows.

### 8.5 ITSM / Service Desk Keywords

Keywords: ITSM vs CMMS, service desk for physical assets, asset service management software, maintenance ticketing system, work order ticketing software.

Angle: SIP is not a generic ticketing system. It connects work orders to assets, maintenance history, documents, parts, inspections, and AI retrieval.
